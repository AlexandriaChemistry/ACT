alexandria::FragmentWall Class Reference

#include <act/properties/fragmentwall.h>

Description

The distance between the compounds of a dimer or cluster, and a wall that keeps them together.

In vacuum, compounds that move apart do not come back. For two compounds (fragments) with the distance R between their centers of mass the wall is the potential

and zero otherwise. For more than two compounds R is the distance of the center of mass of each compound to that of all compounds, and U is the sum over the compounds. The force is divided over the atoms of a compound in proportion to their masses, such that it does not change the structure or the orientation of the compound. The forces add up to zero.
