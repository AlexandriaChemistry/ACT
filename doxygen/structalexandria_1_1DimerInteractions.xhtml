alexandria::DimerInteractions Struct Reference

#include <act/forces/forcecomputer.h>

Description

Interaction energies and forces of the conformations of a dimer The coordinates and forces are stored as in ConformationArrays: component m of particle a of conformation c is at (DIM*a+m)*stride + c.
