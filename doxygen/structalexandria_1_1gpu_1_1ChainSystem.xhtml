size_t alexandria::gpu::ChainSystem::nreplica = 1

The number of replicas, each with its own parameters, steps and chi squared. The molecules are those of all replicas, each knows its replica, see ChainMolecule::replica, the parameters on the GPU are nparam for each replica, one after the other.
