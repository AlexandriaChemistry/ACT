◆ shellsFromInput

bool alexandria::ShellOptions::shellsFromInput = false

With resetShells, whether to start the relaxation from the input positions of the shells. The energies before relaxation, from which the induction follows, are still those with the shells on top of their core. Useful if the input positions are close to the relaxed ones, for instance predicted from the previous step in MD.
