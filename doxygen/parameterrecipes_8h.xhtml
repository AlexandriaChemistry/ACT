Description

Recipes for the parameters of interactions in the units of the program, from the values of the force field.

The parameters of an interaction, as the topology has them, follow from the values of the force field in their own units: either directly, a value times the factor of its unit, see ForceFieldParameter::internalValue, or for pairs of particle types by combining the values of both types with the combination rules, see evalCombinationRule. A recipe says how, for one set of parameters, such as those of a bond type or of a pair of van der Waals types, in terms of the positions of the values in one array. ParameterRecipes makes the recipes and the array, and the parameters can then be computed from the array alone, also on the GPU, with evaluateRecipe, which only uses the functions of combinationkernels.h.
