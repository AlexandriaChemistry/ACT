alexandria::Coordinates Class Reference

#include <act/topology/coords_energy.h>

Description

Integer coordinates of the particles of one structure, owning them The components are stored contiguously, as CoordinateVectors with a stride of one, in units of coordinateUnit, see coordinate.h. Particle a is indexed as x[a][m], like a std::vector<gmx::RVec>, a component reads as a value in nm and the difference of two vectors is exact.
