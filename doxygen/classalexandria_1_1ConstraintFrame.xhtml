alexandria::ConstraintFrame Class Reference

Description

Positions relative to an atom of each group of constrained atoms The constraints are computed with coordinates in double precision in nm, the differences of the integer coordinates, see coordinate.h, relative to one atom of the atoms that constraints connect. They are as precise as double allows for the size of a molecule, in either precision. The constrained particles are put at their anchors plus their relative positions.
