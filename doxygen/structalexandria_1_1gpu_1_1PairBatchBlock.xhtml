Description

The interactions of one potential in the conformations of a molecule, see computePairBlocks The layout is that of computeExplicitSimd: coordinate m of atom a in slot c is x[(3*a+m)*stride + c], in units of coordinateUnit, see coordinate.h, the forces likewise.
