#include <act/topology/coords_energy.h>

Description

template<typename T>
class alexandria::ParticleVectors< T >

Vectors of the particles of one conformation, such as its coordinates or forces, without owning them Component m of particle a is at data[(DIM*a+m)*stride], such that the vectors can be those of one conformation in arrays of many (see ConformationArrays) or contiguous, with a stride of one. Particle a is indexed as v[a][m], like a std::vector<gmx::RVec>.
