alexandria::HessianDiagnostic Struct Reference

#include <act/alexandria/sensitivity.h>

Description

Properties of the full Hessian of the chi-squared The Hessian is scaled by the ranges of the parameters, H_ij r_i r_j, such that its eigenvalues do not depend on the units of the parameters. Positive definite pairs do not make a positive definite Hessian, so this is a stricter test for a minimum than that of all pairs.
