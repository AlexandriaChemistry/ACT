alexandria::NormalMode Struct Reference

#include <act/alexandria/molhandler.h>

Description

The motion of the particles in a normal mode.

The displacements of the atoms are normalized such that the sum of their squares is one, as quantum chemistry programs print them. With l the normalized eigenvector of the mass weighted Hessian, the displacement of atom i is sqrt(mu) l_i/sqrt(m_i), where the reduced mass mu is 1/sum_i(l_i^2/m_i). Note that for a diatomic molecule this is (m1+m2) m1 m2/(m1^2+m2^2) and not m1 m2/(m1+m2).
