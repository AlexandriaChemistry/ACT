Add the dimers of one orientation at all temperatures. Gives the same sums as addData for each dimer and temperature, with the Boltzmann factor g = exp(-beta U) of the energy U of each dimer, g-1 as Mayer function, g times the squared forces and g times the squared torque components, see Gray and Gubbins Eqns. 3.261, 3.272 and 3.282. Temperatures of zero are skipped. Dimers with an energy below eMin are treated as overlapping atoms, with g = 0 as for a strong repulsion, since their energy is not physical. Otherwise the Boltzmann factor can overflow and make the second virial infinite. If the dimers are in the order of their distance indices, the orientation is kept and added to the sums together with the next ones, which is faster, see flush.

Parameters
[in]indexDistance index of each dimer
[in]distDistance between the molecules of each dimer
[in]energyEnergy of each dimer
[in]force2Squared force on each molecule of each dimer
[in]torqueTorque on each molecule of each dimer
[in]weightWeight of each dimer, see DimerGenerator
