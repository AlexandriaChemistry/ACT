Description

template<typename T>
class alexandria::ConformationArrays< T >

Coordinates and forces of the conformations of a topology.

Component m of particle a in slot s is stored at (3*a+m)*stride + s, such that loops over conformations access memory contiguously and can be vectorized. Each conformation has its own slot, see slot. While the shells are relaxed, the conformations for which they did not converge yet are put in the first nslot slots, see arrange, such that only those are computed. Otherwise slot c holds conformation c. The computations use nslot slots, a multiple of the explicit SIMD width. Those beyond the conformations are copies of the last one, see pad, they are computed but not used. The stride, the distance between the rows, is at least the number of conformations rounded up to the width, and one width more if that is a multiple of 256. It is larger when conformations were added one at a time, such that the rows do not have to be moved each time. The coordinates are integers, see coordinate.h, in both precisions.

Put the conformations to compute in the first slots These are the nslot computed slots, the active conformations rounded up to the explicit SIMD width, the conformations that are not active in them are computed as well. Active conformations in other slots are swapped with those that are not active in the computed slots, which saves swaps when few of them converged. The slots of other conformations are swapped with theirs, together with the columns of other arrays in the same order, such as the forces of the entries without shells.
