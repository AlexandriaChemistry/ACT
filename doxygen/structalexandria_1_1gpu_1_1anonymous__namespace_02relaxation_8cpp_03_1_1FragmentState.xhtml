double alexandria::gpu::anonymous_namespace{relaxation.cpp}::FragmentState::fmaxRest

The largest force component on the particles of the fragment that are not shells, at the start, for the rounding floor of the shell forces, see updateShells. During the relaxation only the forces on the shells are computed, those on the other particles change little.
