◆ startPackedBcast()

void alexandria::CommunicationRecord::startPackedBcast ( MPI_Comm comm,
int root = 0 ) const

Pack the broadcasts that follow into a single one Many small broadcasts, such as those of all the data of a molecule, take much longer than a single one with the same data. Until finishPackedBcast is called, the root collects the data of the broadcasts on comm, and the other processes take them from the data they receive here. All processes have to make the same broadcasts in between, and no other broadcasts.

Parameters
[in]commMPI communicator
[in]rootThe root of the broadcasts
