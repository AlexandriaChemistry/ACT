alexandria::ConformationEnergies Class Reference

#include <act/forces/conformationdata.h>

Description

The ACT energies of all conformations, per interaction type This holds the same information as the ACT energies in the energy maps of the conformations: for each interaction type and conformation whether there is an energy, the energy and its backup. The energies of one interaction type are stored contiguously, and are copied to the energy maps by store().
