Description

Sensitivity analysis of a small training set The electronegativities of the atom types of butanol, acetone and water are the parameters, the deviation from the electrostatic potentials of these molecules is the chi-squared.

Run the two-dimensional analysis and compare it to the chi-squared The chi-squared of each corner of each pair is computed here, the coupling is fitted to what the parabolas through the points of each parameter do not describe.

Parameters
[in]paramThe parameters to start from
[out]convergedWhether the analysis found the parameters and all pairs converged, may be nullptr
[out]newtonThe parameters after a projected Newton step, may be nullptr. Parameters at a bound with the gradient pointing out of the bounds stay there, the step of the others uses the pseudo-inverse of their Hessian, and is limited to the bounds.
[out]outputThe output of the analysis, may be nullptr
