alexandria::ShellRelaxation Struct Reference

#include <act/forces/conformationdata.h>

Description

State of the shell relaxation of the conformations, see ForceComputer::updateShells In the layout of ConformationArrays and with the slots of the arrays, such that the loops over the conformations access it in order. Row i of step, force and dxForce holds the values of the shells of fragment i of a dimer.
