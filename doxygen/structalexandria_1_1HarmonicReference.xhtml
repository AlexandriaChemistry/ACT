alexandria::HarmonicReference Struct Reference

#include <act/properties/pathintegralthermo.h>

Description

What the normal mode analysis of the minimized compound gives.

The thermochemistry of the rigid rotor and harmonic oscillators, see ThermoChemistry, which is what the simulations are compared to and where the integrals for the entropy start.
