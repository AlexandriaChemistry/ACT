AverageError alexandria::PathIntegralResults::heatCapacity

The heat capacity at constant volume in kJ/mol K.

From the fluctuations of the energy, with the total energy from the centroid virial estimator and from the primitive estimator , including the translation of the molecule. It has no meaning with a wall, and it converges slowly with the number of beads, see computeThermochemistry for the correction.
