alexandria::ShellForceConstant Struct Reference

#include <act/forcefield/generate_dependent.h>

Description

A shell whose force constant is fixed in the force field.

The force constant of a shell is for shell charge and polarizability . Normally it follows from these two. If the polarization parameters of the shell type have a parameter kshell that is not Dependent, the force constant is given instead, and the one of alpha and the shell charge that is Dependent follows from it and the other one.
