◆ planckScale

double alexandria::RingPolymerSettings::planckScale = 1

The factor by which the constant of Planck is scaled.

With a factor g the beads have the distribution of atoms that are heavier by 1/g^2, a factor that goes to zero gives classical statistics. The integral over g of the kinetic energy gives the difference in free energy between the quantum and the classical molecule, see pathIntegralThermochemistry.

◆ thermostat

Whether to use the thermostat. Without it the Hamiltonian of the ring polymer is conserved, for testing.
