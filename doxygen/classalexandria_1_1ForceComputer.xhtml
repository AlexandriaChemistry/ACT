alexandria::ForceComputer Class Reference

#include <act/forces/forcecomputer.h>

Description

Class to compute all the forces in a molecule or complex. It is imperative that there is only one force computer in each program, since results may depend on the flags given when initiating.

Compute interaction energies of many dimer conformations.

The conformations are computed together with the SIMD routines, see computeRelaxedEnergies. This replaces the conformations stored in the molecule, their energy maps are left empty, see ACTMol::storeEnergies. The output vectors may hold the results of a previous call, their memory is used again.

Parameters
[in]msghandlerFor debugging, may be nullptr
[in]pdThe force field
[in,out]molThe dimer, the conformations are stored in it
[in,out]coordsCoordinates of all particles of each conformation, on return with the relaxed shells
[in]separateInductionCorrectionWhether to store InductionCorrection separately or add it to Induction
[out]einterThe interaction energy terms of each conformation
[out]epotThe potential energy of each dimer conformation
[out]forcesThe interaction forces of each conformation, the forces in the dimer minus those in the monomers

void alexandria::ForceComputer::computeRelaxedEnergies ( MsgHandler * msghandler,
const ForceField * pd,
const std::vector< ACTMol * > & allmols,

bool separateInductionCorrection,

Compute energies and forces for monomers and/or dimers. Despite the name of the function it is possible to select RelaxShells::None which results in a single energy calculation. This will happen in case of a non-polarizable force field. Different molecules and conformation may take different number of iterations, but the intention of the code is to keep running just until the position of the selected shells have been minimized, or maxiter has been reached. The ACT energies are stored in the conformation data of the molecules, see ACTMol::conformationData, not in the energy maps of the conformations. ACTMol::storeEnergies copies them to the energy maps.

Parameters
[in]msghandlerFor debugging and messages
[in]pdAn ACT force field
[in]allmolsThe molecules to compute
[in]resetShellsWhether or not to reset the shell positions before relaxation. This is an optimization that may or may not work.
[in]separateInductionCorrectionWhether or not to treat induction correction separately
[in]computeForcesWhether the forces are needed. If not, they are not computed and the forces of the conformations are not meaningful afterwards. Polarizable force fields always compute forces, since the shells are relaxed with them.

Compute the total energies and forces of many conformations.

The conformations are computed together with the SIMD routines. The virtual sites are constructed, the shells relaxed as in the options and the forces on the virtual sites spread over their parents. The energy terms are those of compute, including INDUCTION, ALLELEC and EXCHIND.

Parameters
[in]msghandlerFor debugging, may be nullptr
[in]pdThe force field
[in,out]setThe conformations, on return with the forces and the relaxed shells
[in]optionsHow to treat the shells, and the electric field
[out]energiesThe energy terms of each conformation
