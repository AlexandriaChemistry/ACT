#include <act/forces/gpusystem.h>

Description

All topologies of the molecules of a process on the GPU There is a block for each topology of each molecule: the monomers and, for dimers, the dimer, as computeEnergies computes them.

With several replicas, all blocks are there once for each replica, each with its own parameters, charges and shells, to compute the molecules for several sets of force field parameters at once: stageParameters takes the parameters of each replica, uploadParameters sends them all, and after computing, selectReplica puts the energies and charges of one replica in the molecules. The bound of the charge of a particle, as BoundsDevComputer::ChargeBound
