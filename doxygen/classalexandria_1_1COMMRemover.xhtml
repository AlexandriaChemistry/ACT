alexandria::COMMRemover Class Reference

#include <act/properties/velocityhandler.h>

Description

Removes the motion of the center of mass The translation is removed by subtracting the velocity of the center of mass, the rotation by subtracting the rigid rotation with the angular momentum around the center of mass. Only particles with a mass take part. For a molecule that is linear in the starting coordinates, the rotation around its axis is not removed, such that the number of degrees of freedom stays the same during the simulation.
