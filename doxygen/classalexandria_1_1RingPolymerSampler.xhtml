alexandria::RingPolymerSampler Class Reference

#include <act/properties/ringpolymer.h>

Description

Path integral molecular dynamics of one molecule.

The quantum mechanical distribution of the atoms of a molecule at a temperature T is sampled as the classical distribution of a ring polymer: P copies (beads) of the molecule, in which each atom is connected to itself in the next bead by a spring with the frequency . The ring polymer is simulated at the temperature PT with the Hamiltonian

With one bead this is classical molecular dynamics.

Only particles with a mass are part of the ring polymer. Shells are relaxed for each bead, and virtual sites are constructed for each bead, such that V is the energy of a bead with relaxed shells. All beads are computed together by the force computer.

The integrator is BAOAB, with the motion in the springs integrated exactly in the normal modes of the free ring polymer (A) and a Langevin thermostat on each normal mode (O), as in the path integral Langevin equation of Ceriotti et al., J. Chem. Phys. 133 (2010) 124104. The thermostat of the internal modes is critically damped, that of the centroid has the time constant of the settings. The center of mass of the centroid does not move.

A potential of the centroids can be added, for instance a wall that keeps the compounds of a dimer together. With the energy U of the centroids , the Hamiltonian of the ring polymer has the term in addition, such that each bead of an atom gets the force on its centroid. This does not change the distribution of the beads around their centroid.
