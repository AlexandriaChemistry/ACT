alexandria::VsiteHandler Class Reference

#include <act/forces/vsitehandler.h>

Description

Class to construct vsite positions and distribute vsite forces.

Periodic boundary conditions are not supported.

Construct the virtual sites of many conformations at once The coordinates are in the layout of ConformationArrays, component m of particle a in slot c is at (3*a+m)*stride + c. The positions are the same as those of constructPositions.

Parameters
[in]topThe molecular topology
[in,out]xThe coordinates, the virtual site positions are set
[in]strideThe stride
[in]nslotThe number of slots to construct, at most stride
[in]nconfThe number of slots that hold conformations, the others are padding, of which the floating point operations are not counted
