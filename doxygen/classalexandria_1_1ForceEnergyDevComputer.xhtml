The deviations of the energies from the energies as the GPU gives them As calcDeviation after ForceComputer::computeRelaxedEnergies on the GPU, without the energies of the conformation data: from ForceComputer::gpuTypeEnergies, with the post-processing of the energies done here. The losses are added to the targets in the same order, such that the results are the same.

Parameters
[in]forceComputerThe force computer, with the energies kept, see ForceComputer::setKeepGpuEnergies
[in]actmolsThe molecules
[in,out]targetsThe fitting targets
[in]lossFunctionThe loss function
Returns
false if this cannot be done for these targets or molecules, then the targets may have changed
