alexandria::CovarianceAverager Class Reference

#include <act/properties/pathintegralmd.h>

Description

Covariance of two series of values with a standard error from blocks.

As BlockAverager, for the covariance of two quantities of a simulation. The covariance is that of all values, the standard error follows from the covariances within the blocks.
