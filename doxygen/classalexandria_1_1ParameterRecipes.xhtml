alexandria::ParameterRecipes Class Reference

#include <act/forcefield/parameterrecipes.h>

Description

The recipes of the parameters of interactions, with the values they use The recipes point to the values of the force field they were made from, which update() copies again, such that the force field must not change its structure afterwards, only its values.
