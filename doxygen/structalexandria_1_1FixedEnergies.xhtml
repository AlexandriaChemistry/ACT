alexandria::FixedEnergies Struct Reference

#include <act/forces/conformationdata.h>

Description

Energies and forces of the entries without shells in all conformations While relaxing the shells, only the entries with shells change, see Topology::separateShells. The others are computed once, in the first calculation of the conformations, and kept here.
